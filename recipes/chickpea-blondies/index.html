Chickpea Blondies

Noone can tell that these chickpea blondies have beans in them. The peanut butter and chocolate mask any bean flavor. A tasty way to sneak some fiber into your kids' diet.

Ingredients

  • 1 (15-ounce) can chickpeas, rinsed and drained
  • 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup all purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup semisweet chocolate chips
  • 2 tablespoons semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt

Directions

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Line an 8x8-inch baking pan with parchment paper.

  2. Add chickpeas to a food processor. Process until nearly smooth, Add peanut butter, brown sugar, flour, vanilla, baking soda, and salt. Process until smooth. Mixture will be very thick. Stir in 1/2 cup chocolate chips.

  3. Spread dough evenly in prepared pan. Top with remaining 2 tablespoons chocolate chips.

  4. Bake until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean, 25 to 28 minutes.

  5. Sprinkle with flaky sea salt

  6. Cool in pan on a wire rack 30 minutes. Remove from pan and cool completely on the wire rack, 15 minutes. Cut in squares.
